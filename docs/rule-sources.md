## Verified examples

The Adult announcement p. 11 permits at most four Bronze jump elements, prohibits Axels and specifies at most two occurrences of a listed jump. It distinguishes falls inside elements from falls outside them for deductions. Communication 2788 pp. 14 and 16 specifies a GOE reduction and a starting-GOE limit for falls; a reduction is not automatically the final GOE. The simulator must keep TES and program deductions separate. These examples do not establish complete compliance or substitute for the documents.

## Open before implementation

- Confirm all applicable amendments and event-specific documents, precedence and effective dates for each target competition. The announcement alone does not establish rules for all adult competitions.
- Resolve exact treatment and interaction of calls, invalid or excess elements, GOE, level caps, rounding and deductions using the applicable clauses and reviewed official examples. Identify the calculation authority for each step.
- Establish source-backed jump-transition and spin-feature dependencies; do not infer physical feasibility from notation alone.
- Define finite optimizer candidates, program equivalence and completeness/limit reporting with domain review. A solver's optimum applies only to the modeled domain.
- Obtain independently checked fixtures, including official protocols where suitable and legally usable, and human domain review before claiming scoring correctness.
